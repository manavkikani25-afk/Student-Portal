Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my portal password?

You can click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email address.

Where can I view my attendance?

Once logged in, navigate to the "Attendance" section from the dashboard to view your complete attendance records.

How do I pay my semester fees online?

Go to the "Fees" section in your dashboard, select the pending fee, and click on "Pay Now" to complete the payment via our secure gateway.

What should I do if my timetable is incorrect?

Please contact your department coordinator or the university administration immediately to report any discrepancies in your timetable.

Can I apply for leave through the portal?

Yes, you can apply for leave by going to the "Leave Applications" tab on your dashboard and filling out the required details.

Need Help?

Contact the university administration for technical or academic support.
